# Eight catalogued packs now stored

All eight previously unstored packs were retrieved from official creator distributions and stored in [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/atiffhussainkhan/Image/releases/tag/catalog-completion-2026-10-06). The legacy KayKit Dungeon archive is distinct from the remastered pack. Only the free CC0 Nature & Terrain tier is included.

Every ZIP member passed CRC checking, every payload was hashed and checked for nonzero size, raster images were decoded, and model headers were checked. These checks establish file integrity; engine imports were not run. Original licence files and provenance are retained. Detailed per-file hashes are in validation.json.

# Licence and provenance policy

1. Every upstream pack must have a traceable source page and an explicit licence.
2. Prefer CC0/public-domain assets for frictionless reuse.
3. MIT/BSD assets are allowed only when their required copyright/licence notice remains with the redistributed files.
4. Do not import assets with unclear, contradictory or unverifiable terms.
5. Do not strip upstream README, LICENSE or attribution files.
6. Do not relabel third-party work as repository-owned.
7. Record the original source URL, creator, licence and relevant version/date where available.
8. Before committing a downloaded archive, inspect it for its own licence/README and retain those files next to the asset.
9. Avoid duplicate packs and low-quality filler.
10. If a pack has a free and paid tier, catalogue and import only the clearly licensed free tier unless separately acquired and redistribution is permitted.

The catalogue's `license` field is a retrieval aid, not a substitute for the upstream licence text.
